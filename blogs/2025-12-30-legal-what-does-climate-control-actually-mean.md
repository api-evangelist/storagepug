Key points There is no legal definition of "climate control" for self storage. Provide an accurate definition in your rental agreement Communicate what your climate control does to the tenant Don't try to oversell - be clear and consistent in your definition. Nevada requires operators to include temp and humidity ranges when advertising climate control!
