Portable storage boomed in the last decade because it solves a problem that standard storage facilities can’t touch. The portable storage industry grew by 2.6% to $8 billion in revenue in 2018 - because of this growth, many self storage operators are considering whether adding portable storage might grow their business. Can portable storage make your business stand out, or is it a money sink that won’t pay for itself?
